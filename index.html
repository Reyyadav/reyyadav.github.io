How I report results

Most of my work is deciding what a number is allowed to claim.

Association vs. causation

A well-designed randomized experiment can support causal inference. A post-exposure matched comparison remains observational. I distinguish these designs and communicate uncertainty.

Denominators first

I reconcile counts across snapshots and check join cardinality before any figure leaves my notebook, so two numbers never come from different populations.

Guardrails with the headline

Every primary metric ships with the metrics that must not get worse, such as latency, failures and cancellations.
